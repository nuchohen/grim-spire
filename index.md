# Grim Spire: The Evolving RPG Adventure (Fabric 1.20.1)

**Grim Spire** is a custom-built RPG adventure designed for players who value freedom and deep immersion.  
Unlike many packs, there are **no artificial resource locks** — you can explore and gather at your own pace from the very beginning.  
The focus is on **world evolution** and a **distinct visual style**, crafted with meticulous attention to detail by a creator with a graphic design background.

**Version:** Minecraft **1.20.1 (Fabric)**

## ⚔️ Key Features

### 🧱 Staged Difficulty (Terraria-style progression)
The world of Grim Spire is constantly evolving. Defeating key bosses doesn’t just check a box — it **changes the world**.  
New threats, events, and environmental challenges emerge as you progress, forcing your team to adapt and grow stronger.

### 🧩 Total Freedom & Flexible Roles
Forget rigid class systems that lock you into one playstyle forever. Grim Spire is built around adaptability:

- **Dynamic swapping:** Change gear and builds on the fly to meet the moment  
- **Team synergy:** Be the shield in a boss fight, then swap to support/utility when needed

### 🎨 Bespoke Hand-Drawn HUD
The interface is the soul of the experience. Drawing from professional design experience, the HUD is **fully hand-crafted** to be both informative and aesthetically striking.

### 🧬 Genetic Evolution
Discover unique genes, combine them, and unlock passive buffs or active abilities that redefine how you play.

### 🌍 High-Quality Localization & Sound
Enjoy a seamless experience with high-quality translation and atmosphere-focused sound design: ambient audio + music that makes the world feel “alive”.

---

## 👥 Multiplayer & Survival
Grim Spire was built with a **Multiplayer-First** philosophy.  
Success depends not just on gear, but on coordination and smart role switching. Explore a world where difficulty scales with achievements — every victory feels earned.
